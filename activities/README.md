# Achieving Sales Goals with Customer-Centric Selling Techniques — Activities

Four in-class **role plays** — one per Learning Unit. Each uses a realistic, self-contained Singapore sales scenario (Sunrise Builders, Northlight Retail, Harbourfront Living and a Bukit Timah homeowner) and practises a one-to-one sales conversation built on the course's discovery, closing and objection-handling techniques.

## What each activity folder contains

| Artefact | What it is | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| `ANN-Facilitator-Guide-*.docx` / `.pdf` | Purpose, materials, set-up, scenario, the numbered run-sheet with facilitator notes, timing, debrief and success criterion | Trainer |
| `ANN-Learner-Worksheet-*.docx` / `.pdf` | The scenario, the steps, and a working space to capture the artefact and prompt answers | Learner |
| `ANN-Checklist-*.docx` / `.pdf` | Criteria table to tick before the debrief, plus feedback | Learner group |

> The **detailed step-by-step instructions live here and in the Learner Guide — never on the slides.** The trainer facilitates the scenario, roles and discussion from the deck while learners work from these sheets.

## Course structure

- **LU1 — Foundations of Selling** (Activities 1–1): Mindset · Empathy · Sales Strategy · Buyer Psychology · Discovery
- **LU2 — Selling Techniques & Problem-Solving** (Activities 2–2): Problem-Based Selling · Demos · Upselling & Cross-Selling · Personalisation · Champions
- **LU3 — Closing the Sale** (Activities 3–3): Buying Signals · Closing Techniques · Justifying Price · Easy to Buy · Follow-Up
- **LU4 — Handling Objections & Rejections** (Activities 4–4): Objection vs Deflection vs Rejection · Answer & Ask · Composure · Testimonials · Metrics
